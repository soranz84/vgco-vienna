Construction starts, ends and durations

Each site gets a start (the first year it shows activity) and an end (the year the building is finished). Start = your manually recorded start if you set one, otherwise the Sentinel-2 onset. End = your recorded end if you set one, otherwise the first orthophoto vintage that shows the structure. A site with a start and no end is ongoing.

Read the numbers like this. The headline is building-footprint area — not construction-site area, not gross floor area. Completed is the orthophoto's series and it has holes in 2016, 2017, 2018, 2021 and 2024, because only seven of the twelve available vintages can be voted in. Detected is the Sentinel-2 series that fills those holes; it is a floor, not an estimate, because it only finds constructions that cover at least three of a 3×3 pixel neighbourhood (≈300 m²) and persist for two annual observations. The median official footprint is 51 m² — half of one Sentinel-2 pixel — so small constructions are structurally invisible to the satellite and invisible to this table. Measured, not asserted: in a district the engine had never seen (Währing), it fired on 6.0 % of the footprints that actually changed (90 % CI 3.7–11.5 %) while 83 % of what it flagged was real. No threshold, cluster floor or area floor lifts that recall above ~17 % — the ceiling is the 10 m pixel. So read every Sentinel-2 number here as a floor, and the district comparison as a comparison of detectable activity, not of total activity.
Accuracy, measured not asserted. Sentinel-2 false-positive rate 0.77% on 13,797 footprints the orthophoto saw standing in 2014. On the 19 human-labelled sites: 8 of 13 construction sites detected at the right year, 1 of 6 not-construction sites falsely detected — precision 89%, recall 62%. 0.77% false-positive rate on 13,797 pre-2014 control footprints (12,527 m2 wrongly counted).

The continuous series

The red bands are the orthophoto's blind years. The Sentinel-2 series (orange) continues through them, but it is a floor: it counts only large, unambiguous events, and it does not agree with the orthophoto year-for-year because it measures a different thing — the ground change, not the structure.
