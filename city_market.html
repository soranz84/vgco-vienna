The false-positive rate is measured on the footprints that already showed a building in the 2014 orthophoto. It is an upper bound, because a new floor on an old building is real construction that the test cannot distinguish from a spurious firing.
The series is a floor. The detector fires on about six per cent of the footprints that actually changed in a district it has never seen, at the frozen setting, and on about a fifth when the false-positive guarantee is spent on sensitivity. A district with no detections is therefore not a district without construction.
