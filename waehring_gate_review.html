VGCO — the Währing gate labels

The recall half of the scaling gate. 25 footprints, a STRATIFIED sample of the Währing population, so the verdicts give an unbiased recall and precision with known weights. Each card shows the current flight over the plot and the same ground in every flight since 2014. The question: is there construction here, and when?

How to answer. Put yes, no or unsure in the construction column of labels/waehring_gate_labels.csv, and the year of the works in year. The strata: D = the engine fired here (the precision of the positives), A = the orthophoto dates an appearance after 2014 and the engine was silent (the missed detections), N = nothing appeared and the engine was silent (the blind spot). An unsure is worth more than a guess.
