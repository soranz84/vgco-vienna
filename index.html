VGCO — Vienna Ground-Change Observatory

A motion-aware, geotechnically contextualised observatory of construction sites in Vienna, built from official footprints, Vienna's orthophoto (2014–2025), Sentinel-2 (2015–2026), Sentinel-1 and the EGMS InSAR motion field. Every number carries its measured accuracy and its coverage.

The interactive product is one web app

Run streamlit run app.py and it opens with two tabs: Label (the site map, the orthophoto carousel and the annotation form) and Candidates (the detector's work-list, also labelable); the Market dashboard is the second view. The pages below are the static twin, so the same evidence can be read without a server.
